Financial Plan — Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

Financial Plan is a personal financial planner built to work entirely in your browser. Because the numbers you put in are about as private as data gets, this page explains exactly what exists, where it goes, and who can see it.

What Financial Plan stores

Only what you type in: budget figures, account balances, savings goals, retirement and compensation figures, vacation costs, PTO plans, donations, and any notes — plus your preferences (theme, which tab was open). There is no account, profile, or usage tracking required to use the app.

Where that data lives

In your browser (always). Everything is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This is the only copy that exists unless you sign in to sync. Clearing the site's browser data — or the app's own "Delete all data" button — deletes it.

Google sign-in / Firestore (optional, off until you choose it). If you tap "Sign in to sync", your data is stored in a Firebase/Firestore database operated by the developer so the same figures can appear on your other devices, and Firebase Authentication records your Google account's email address and display name. Access rules restrict every account to its own data — other signed-in users cannot read yours, and the developer does not look at it. This only happens if you sign in; the app never does it on its own. "Delete all data" while signed in also removes the synced copy.

Holding price lookups (optional, off until you add a key). If you enter an Alpha Vantage API key in Preferences, the Investments tab can fetch current prices for the tickers you have recorded (any holding whose price lookup you have left switched on). Only the ticker symbol and your own key are sent to alphavantage.co — never a balance, a share count, or anything else about you. The key is kept in this browser only: it is deliberately not part of your synced data and not part of an exported backup, so you enter it once per device. Fetched prices are cached for six hours and remain editable by hand. Delete the key in Preferences to switch lookups off.

What Financial Plan does not do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking scripts
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing data with third parties
  • Sharing the app's address shares only the app — never your numbers
  • The "Recent changes" list fetches this app's own public commit history from GitHub's API; nothing about you is sent with that request

Your choices

You can edit or delete anything in the app at any time, and export a full backup from the Back up dialog. Signing out stops all syncing and leaves the local copy in charge. To have a synced copy deleted from the developer's Firestore database, use "Delete all data" while signed in, or email eagleadams86@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
